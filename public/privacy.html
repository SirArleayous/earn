Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At SINEX, we take privacy and data governance seriously, strictly adhering to regional data protection frameworks including Nigeria's NDPR and international standards.

1. Data We Collect

We collect identity details (name, email, phone number), KYC verification documents (National Identification Numbers, Government IDs), GPS coordinates for field tasks, and wallet transaction history.

2. How We Use Your Data

Data is used exclusively for identity verification, anti-fraud evaluation, field task geotagging, payout processing, and campaign result aggregation.
