Two Ecosystems, One Seamless Platform

Discover how users earn and businesses execute campaigns across African markets.

For Earners

  1. Create Free Account: Sign up and complete initial identity verification.
  2. Browse Marketplace: Select surveys, mobile tests, or field tasks matching your location.
  3. Submit Evidence: Upload receipts, survey inputs, or media files.
  4. Get Paid: Instant wallet updates upon approval with direct mobile money/bank withdrawal.

For Businesses

  1. Launch Campaign: Set parameters, evidence rules, and country targets.
  2. Fund Escrow: Deposit funds into secure campaign wallet.
  3. Real-time Results: Automated & manual verification of incoming task evidence.
  4. Export Analytics: Access rich, structured datasets and demographic insights.
